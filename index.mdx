Screening is the infrastructure layer for independent film distribution. Whether you're running a one-night premiere in a community hall, streaming to an international audience, or doing both at once, Screening handles ticketing, live streaming, door check-in, and direct payouts — so you focus on the film.

## Get started in four steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request host access">
    Screening is invite-only for filmmakers. Submit a request at [usescreening.com/host](https://usescreening.com/host) and the team will review your application.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Stripe and set up your organisation">
    Sign in to [Studio](https://usescreening.com/account), accept the host terms, and connect your Stripe account so you can receive payouts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your film and schedule a screening night">
    Create a film record with title, runtime, and poster. Then schedule a screening — choose online, in-person, or hybrid. Upload your film file for online playback.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish and share">
    Publish the screening to open the ticket booth at `usescreening.com/{org}/{screening}`. Share the link, and buyers pay directly to you through Stripe Checkout.
  </Step>
</Steps>
