Screening is home to ticketed premiere nights and on-demand independent films — and finding them is designed to be frictionless whether you arrive through a filmmaker's direct link or browse on your own. You can discover screenings publicly on the Discover feed, follow the filmmakers and organisations you care about, or jump straight to a booth if someone sent you a link. No account is required at any step.

## The Discover Feed

The Discover feed at [/discover](https://usescreening.com/discover) lists upcoming published screenings that are visible to the public. Screenings are listed on Discover by default — filmmakers can choose to unlist a night and share it privately by direct link only, so not everything appears here.

Each card on Discover shows the film poster, title, organisation, showtime, format, and ticket price so you can decide at a glance whether a night is right for you.

## Organisation Pages

Every filmmaker or production company on Screening has a public organisation page at `/{org}` (for example, `usescreening.com/neonwave`). Visit an org page to see:

- The organisation's upcoming scheduled nights
- Past screenings they have hosted
- Films available to rent or buy on-demand
- A **Follow** button to subscribe to their updates

Click **Follow** on any org page to receive notifications when the filmmaker schedules a new night. You'll get an email the moment they open ticket sales.

## Direct Booth Links

Filmmakers frequently share a direct link to their screening booth — `usescreening.com/{org}/{slug}` — through social media, newsletters, or word of mouth. If you have one of these links, you don't need to visit Discover at all. Click the link and you land straight on the screening's poster, showtime, and buy button.

<Tip>
  If a filmmaker sent you a link and you can't find the screening on Discover, that's intentional — some filmmakers keep their night unlisted and share it only with their own audience.
</Tip>

## Interest Pages and Waitlists

Before tickets go on sale, filmmakers can open an interest page at `/interest/{slug}`. Joining the waitlist here signals your intent to buy and means you'll be notified the moment the booth opens. Look out for interest page links in filmmaker newsletters and social posts ahead of an announced premiere.

## Following Organisations

You can follow an organisation from:

- The org's public page at `/{org}` — click **Follow**
- Your ticket portal at [/me](https://usescreening.com/me) after you have bought a ticket — find the filmmaker there and hit **Follow**

Once you follow an organisation, Screening emails you when they publish a new screening.

<Note>
  You don't need an account to browse Discover, visit an org page, or buy a ticket. Accounts are entirely optional — they give you a persistent ticket library at [/me](https://usescreening.com/me) across devices, but browsing and purchasing work with just your email.
</Note>
