Your ticket portal lives at [usescreening.com/me](https://usescreening.com/me). It's the one place to see every ticket tied to your email, retrieve ticket codes and QR codes, check upcoming screenings, and review your watch history — all without creating an account. If you want a persistent library across devices, you can optionally create a buyer account, but it's never required.

## Looking Up Your Tickets Without an Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to /me">
    Open [usescreening.com/me](https://usescreening.com/me) in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your purchase email">
    Type the email address you used when you bought your ticket. This is the same address your confirmation email was sent to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your inbox for the magic link">
    Screening sends a one-time sign-in link to that email. Open your inbox, find the email from Screening, and click the link. You don't need a password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View your tickets">
    Your ticket portal opens, showing all tickets tied to that email — upcoming screenings, past watch history, and on-demand rentals or purchases.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Downloading Your Ticket

Your ticket code and QR code are available in two places:

1. **Confirmation email** — sent immediately after purchase. It contains your full ticket code and a QR image you can save to your camera roll or print.
2. **Your portal at /me** — log in with your purchase email any time to retrieve or download the code again.

If you're attending an in-person or hybrid screening, either the QR code or the ticket code is sufficient for door entry — door staff can scan the QR or search your code in the check-in tool.

## If You've Lost Your Confirmation Email

Go to [/me](https://usescreening.com/me), enter your purchase email, and Screening will send you a fresh magic link to access your tickets. From there you can view your ticket code and QR code exactly as they appeared in the original confirmation.

<Tip>
  Check your spam or junk folder if your confirmation email doesn't arrive within a few minutes of purchase. Add `hello@usescreening.com` to your contacts to make sure future emails land in your inbox.
</Tip>

## Adding a Screening to Your Calendar

You can add any upcoming screening to your calendar from two places:

- **The thanks page** — immediately after checkout, a calendar download link appears on the confirmation screen
- **Your ticket detail at /me** — open any upcoming ticket and use the **Add to calendar** option

Calendar files work with Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook, and any app that supports the `.ics` format.

## Following Organisations from /me

After supporting a filmmaker, you can follow their organisation directly from your ticket portal:

1. Open any ticket from a past screening
2. Find the organisation name on the ticket detail
3. Click **Follow** — you'll receive an email notification when they publish their next screening

You can manage all your follows from the main /me view and unfollow at any time.

## Creating a Buyer Account (Optional)

Creating a buyer account at [/me](https://usescreening.com/me) is entirely optional. An account lets you:

- Stay signed in across devices without re-entering your email each time
- Keep your full ticket library and watch history in one persistent place
- Manage follows without re-authenticating

Sign up with your email and a password at [/login](https://usescreening.com/login). Use the same email you've used for past purchases so your ticket history carries over.

<Note>
  A buyer account at **/me is separate from filmmaker (host) access**. Creating a buyer account does not give you access to Studio or any filmmaker tools. If you are a filmmaker looking to host screenings, sign up separately at [host.usescreening.com/signup](https://host.usescreening.com/signup).
</Note>
