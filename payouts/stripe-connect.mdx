Screening uses **Stripe Connect Express** to route ticket revenue directly to you. When a buyer checks out, their payment goes straight to your connected Stripe account — not through Screening first. Screening collects its platform fee (5% + 30p) as an application fee deducted at the point of sale. You are the merchant of record: your name appears on the buyer's card statement, and Stripe pays out your bank account on their standard schedule.

<Warning>
  You cannot publish paid screenings until your Stripe account is connected and Stripe has activated your `card_payments` capability. The booth will stay in a payouts-not-ready state until verification is complete. Free (£0) screenings are unaffected.
</Warning>

## How to connect Stripe

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Studio Money">
    Sign in to Studio at [usescreening.com/account](https://usescreening.com/account) and navigate to **Money** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the Stripe onboarding flow">
    Click **Connect Stripe**. Screening redirects you to Stripe's hosted onboarding flow. For UK accounts, this typically takes around five minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Stripe's requirements">
    Stripe will ask for:
    - **Business details** — your name, address, and type of business (individual or company)
    - **Bank account** — the account where payouts land
    - **Identity verification** — a government-issued ID, and in some cases additional documentation

    Complete every step Stripe presents. Skipping or deferring requirements leaves your account in a restricted state.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to Studio">
    After submitting, Stripe redirects you back to Studio. Screening checks your account status automatically. Once Stripe activates your `card_payments` capability, your account is ready and you can publish paid screenings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Checking your connect status

Studio shows a live status badge in **Money**. The underlying API endpoint is:

```
GET /api/v1/host/connect/status
```

The response includes four fields:

| Field | What it means |
|---|---|
| `chargesEnabled` | Your account can accept card payments |
| `payoutsEnabled` | Stripe can send funds to your bank account |
| `detailsSubmitted` | You have completed Stripe's onboarding form |
| `ready` | All three above are `true` — you can sell tickets |

Until `ready` is `true`, Screening blocks ticket sales on paid screenings.

## Accessing your Stripe Dashboard

Once connected, click **Open Stripe Dashboard** in Studio → Money. From there you can:

- View your payout schedule and history
- See individual transaction details
- Review any disputes or chargebacks
- Update your bank account or business details

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The Connect button returns me to Studio but status still shows 'not ready'">
    Stripe may have additional requirements to verify before activating `card_payments`. Open your Stripe Dashboard and look for an **action required** banner — there is usually a specific item (e.g. upload an ID document or confirm your address) that needs completing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Stripe says my account is 'restricted'">
    A restricted account has an active `card_payments` requirement that Stripe is waiting on. Check your Stripe Dashboard for outstanding items. Requirements can include identity re-verification, proof of address, or additional business documents.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I'm not based in the UK — can I still connect?">
    Yes. Stripe Connect Express supports multiple countries. Screening is GBP-first at checkout, but Stripe settles into your local payout currency. If your country is not supported by Stripe Express, you will not be able to complete onboarding — [check Stripe's supported countries list](https://stripe.com/global) before requesting host access.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  Screening never stores your bank details. All financial information is held and processed by Stripe. If you need to update your bank account, do so directly in your Stripe Dashboard.
</Note>
