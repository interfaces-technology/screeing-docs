Screening is film infrastructure for independent filmmakers. It lets you premiere your film to the world — in a room, online, or both at the same time — and keep the same film available to rent or buy long after the night ends. Buyers pay you directly through Stripe. Screening handles ticketing, live streaming, email reminders, and on-demand access in one flow, so you can self-release without a distributor, a cinema deal, or a patchwork of separate tools.

## The two products

Every filmmaker on Screening gets access to two complementary products that together replace the distributor's job for a self-releasing indie: launch with an event, then keep earning after the credits roll.

| Product | What it is | Who pays |
|---|---|---|
| **Screening night** | A ticketed event — online, in-person, or hybrid | Ticket buyer at checkout |
| **On-demand** | Rent (timed) or buy (keep) the film on its catalogue page | Catalogue buyer at any time |

Both products run on the same payout rails and the same playback protection. A premiere is simply a screening night marked as opening night — it is a label, not a separate product.

## The hybrid premiere model

Most tools force a choice: cinema *or* stream. Screening treats one night as three audiences at once. When you schedule a **hybrid** screening, buyers choose at checkout whether they're attending in person or watching online — one showtime, two ways to attend, one set of sales.

| Format | Room | Online | Typical use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `online` | — | ✓ | Remote premieres, diaspora audiences, global day-one |
| `in_person` | ✓ | — | Venue nights, community screenings, pop-ups |
| `hybrid` | ✓ | ✓ | Red carpet in the room, premiere everywhere else |

The in-person QR code does not unlock the stream, and the stream link does not replace the door. Each format is independently gated.

## How money flows

Buyers pay you — the filmmaker — not Screening. You are the merchant of record via **Stripe Connect Express**. Screening applies a platform fee at checkout and passes the rest directly to your connected Stripe account.

<Note>
  **The fee is 5% + 30p per paid ticket, all-in.** Card processing is absorbed inside that rate — there is no separate Stripe fee on top for standard sales. Free tickets collect no fee at all.

  On a **£10 ticket**: buyer pays £10 → Screening takes 80p → you receive £9.20.
</Note>

There is no monthly subscription on the Free tier. You only pay when you sell a ticket. Platform admins can waive the Screening cut for an organisation or a specific night where appropriate.

## What Screening is not

Screening has deliberate scope. Understanding what falls outside it helps set the right expectations before you build your release strategy around it.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Not Eventbrite for any event">
    Screening is for independent film only — features, documentaries, shorts, and series. It is not a general-purpose ticketing platform for conferences, gigs, or other live events.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Not a streaming service">
    Screening does not programme a Netflix-style homepage of other people's films, offer a cross-filmmaker bundle, or run a viewer subscription. Every film lives on its own booth under the filmmaker's organisation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Not open self-serve hosting">
    Hosting is invite-only. You request access and Screening reviews your application. This keeps quality high and support manageable during the current phase.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Not a refund platform">
    All sales are final in v1 — there is no in-app refund button for hosts. Chargebacks and exceptional cases are handled outside the platform via Stripe directly.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Not a festival operating system">
    Screening does not manage submissions, jury deliberations, award ceremonies, or multi-programmer curation. It is infrastructure for distribution, not festival operations.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
