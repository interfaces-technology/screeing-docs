Screening is open self-serve. Any filmmaker can create a host account, set up an organisation, and publish a screening without an invite or manual approval. Sign up at **[host.usescreening.com/signup](https://host.usescreening.com/signup)** and you land in Studio as soon as your email is confirmed.

## Create your account

Go to **[host.usescreening.com/signup](https://host.usescreening.com/signup)** and sign up with your email address and a password. Screening sends a confirmation email. Click the link to verify your address, then sign in at [host.usescreening.com/account](https://host.usescreening.com/account). You can add a passkey afterwards to skip the password on future logins.

<Note>
  Signup lands on a "confirm your email" screen rather than logging you straight in. You have to click the link in that email before you can access Studio.
</Note>

## Onboarding flow

Once your email is confirmed, follow these steps to finish setting up your host account:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your organisation">
    In Studio, create your organisation and choose its URL slug (for example `usescreening.com/yourname`). This is the identity buyers see on your booth and on Discover. The slug is reserved as soon as you claim it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept host terms">
    Review and accept the Screening Organiser Agreement. You'll find it in **Studio → Money**. Sales stay closed until you've accepted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Stripe">
    Go to **Studio → Money → Connect Stripe**. Screening uses Stripe Connect Express to process payments — you are the merchant of record, and buyers pay you directly. You'll need a bank account, a phone number for verification, and your business or personal details ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in your public profile">
    Add your organisation name, avatar, and bio. This is what shows on your booth and on Discover. You can update your profile at any time from **Studio → Money**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Publishing a paid screening requires a Stripe account with charges enabled, a signed Organiser Agreement, and a playable stream. Studio shows a checklist on the Home tab so you can see what's left. Free (£0) screenings do not need Stripe.
</Note>

## Roles and permissions

When you sign up, you become the **owner** of your organisation. You can then invite team members with more limited roles. Every role is scoped to a single organisation.

| Role | What they can do |
|------|-----------------|
| **Owner** | Full access — billing, team, delete org |
| **Admin** | Everything except billing and team management |
| **Editor** | Create and edit films and screenings, but not publish |
| **Viewer** | Read-only access to Studio |
| **Door** | Door check-in view only — no CMS access |

<Tip>
  One login can be both a host and a buyer. Use your host account to purchase tickets for other filmmakers' nights. Your tickets appear in the buyer-facing [/me](https://usescreening.com/me) page.
</Tip>
