Screening is invite-only for filmmakers. To run ticketed nights and sell on-demand access, you need a host account — start by submitting a request at [usescreening.com/host](https://usescreening.com/host). Once the team reviews and approves your request, you'll receive an invite to set up your Studio workspace and connect your bank through Stripe.

## Request host access

Submit your access request at **[usescreening.com/host](https://usescreening.com/host)**. The Screening team reviews every application and reaches out by email when your invite is ready. There is no fixed timeline, but most requests are reviewed within a few days.

## Onboarding flow

Once your invite arrives, follow these steps to finish setting up your host account:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request access">
    Go to [usescreening.com/host](https://usescreening.com/host) and submit your filmmaker details. The Screening team will review your request and email you an invite when you're approved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept your invite and sign up">
    Click the link in your invite email to open Studio at [/account](https://usescreening.com/account). Create your account with an email address and password. You can also add a passkey after sign-up to log in without a password in future.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept host terms">
    Review and accept the Screening host terms. You'll find these in **Studio → Money**. Sales remain closed until you've accepted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Stripe">
    Go to **Studio → Money → Connect Stripe**. Screening uses Stripe Connect Express to process payments — you are the merchant of record, and buyers pay you directly. You'll need a bank account, a phone number for verification, and your business or personal details ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your organisation URL and profile">
    Choose your organisation URL (for example `usescreening.com/yourname`) and fill in your public profile. This is the identity buyers see on your booth and on Discover. You can update your profile at any time from **Studio → Settings**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  All five onboarding steps must be complete before you can publish a screening and open ticket sales. Studio shows a checklist on the Home tab so you can see what's left.
</Note>

## Roles and permissions

When you sign up through an invite, you become the **owner** of your organisation. You can then invite team members with more limited roles. Every role is scoped to a single organisation.

| Role | What they can do |
|------|-----------------|
| **Owner** | Full access — billing, team, delete org |
| **Admin** | Everything except billing and team management |
| **Editor** | Create and edit films and screenings, but not publish |
| **Viewer** | Read-only access to Studio |
| **Door** | Door check-in view only — no CMS access |

<Tip>
  One login can be both a host and a buyer. Use your host account to purchase tickets for other filmmakers' nights — your tickets appear in the buyer-facing [/me](https://usescreening.com/me) page.
</Tip>

## What happens at each stage

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="While you wait" icon="hourglass">
    Your access request sits in the Screening team's inbox. There's no action needed on your side — you'll receive an email when your invite is ready.
  </Card>
  <Card title="After approval" icon="envelope-open">
    You receive an email with a sign-up link. The link is single-use and expires, so complete sign-up when you receive it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="After sign-up" icon="building">
    You're an organisation owner with a Studio workspace. Your organisation URL is reserved. You cannot publish a screening yet — complete the onboarding checklist first.
  </Card>
  <Card title="After onboarding" icon="circle-check">
    You can add films, schedule nights, and publish your first screening. Buyers can find you on Discover or via your direct booth link.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
