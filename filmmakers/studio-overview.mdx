Studio is your filmmaker command centre, available at [/account](https://usescreening.com/account). From a single workspace you manage your entire catalogue, schedule and publish screenings, track revenue and payouts, look after your audience, and run door check-in on the night — without switching between separate tools. Every section is built around the film-first model: you add a film once, then schedule as many nights from it as you like.

## Studio areas

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Home" icon="house">
    Your at-a-glance dashboard. See your next upcoming screening, the onboarding activation checklist, and suggested next actions. Before you've completed onboarding, Home surfaces exactly what's blocking you from going live.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Films" icon="film">
    Your catalogue. Add a new film, upload a poster and stream file, configure on-demand rent and buy pricing, and schedule screening nights directly from a film record. Films are the canonical entity in Studio — create each film once and reuse it across multiple nights.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Nights" icon="calendar">
    A calendar view of all your screenings. Open any night to edit its details, change the status, publish it, access the share kit, or switch to the door check-in view. Nights you haven't published yet appear as Coming Soon to buyers.
  </Card>
  <Card title="People" icon="users">
    Your audience list. View everyone who has bought a ticket, followed your organisation, or signed up to a waitlist. Use People to understand demand and keep in touch with your audience.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Money" icon="sterling-sign">
    Sales totals, a breakdown of ticket revenue versus on-demand (rent/buy) revenue, payout history, and your Stripe Connect settings. Host terms acceptance also lives here — you must accept before publishing your first screening.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Team" icon="user-plus">
    Invite collaborators and assign roles. Send invites by email and choose from owner, admin, editor, viewer, or door. Door staff only see the Door section — they have no access to the CMS, sales data, or audience list.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Door" icon="door-open">
    Web-based ticket check-in for in-person and hybrid screenings. Search by ticket code or scan QR codes from buyers' confirmation emails using a device camera. No app installation needed — Door works in any modern browser.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Sign-in and security

Sign in at [/account](https://usescreening.com/account) with your email address and password. Screening supports **passkeys** — set one up in your account settings to sign in with Face ID, Touch ID, or a hardware security key instead of a password.

<Tip>
  Passkeys are faster and more secure than passwords. Set one up in **Studio → Account** after your first sign-in.
</Tip>

## Share kit

Every published screening comes with a share kit you can access from the night detail view in Studio:

| Asset | What it is |
|-------|-----------|
| **Booth link** | Your primary public URL: `usescreening.com/{org}/{slug}` |
| **Short link** | A compact redirect: `usescreening.com/r/{code}` — useful for social posts and printed materials |
| **Invite text** | Pre-written copy you can paste into social posts, emails, or a group chat |
| **QR code** | A downloadable QR image that links to your booth — print it on posters or slides |
| **Calendar file** | A `.ics` download that adds your screening to any calendar app |

## Roles at a glance

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Owner" icon="crown">
    Full access including billing, team management, and deleting the organisation.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Admin" icon="shield">
    Everything the owner can do except billing and team management.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Editor" icon="pencil">
    Create and edit films and screenings. Cannot publish or manage billing.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Viewer" icon="eye">
    Read-only access to Studio. Useful for press or business partners who need to see data.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Door" icon="door-open">
    Door check-in only. Sees the Door nav and nothing else — no films, no sales figures.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
