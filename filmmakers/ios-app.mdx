**Screening for Hosts** is the native iOS app for filmmakers and door staff. It signs in with your usual Studio account, pulls your upcoming screenings, and gives you everything you need on the night: door check-in (search or QR scan), in-person ticket sales via **Tap to Pay on iPhone**, in-app screening creation from a film in your library, and an Insights tab for lifetime totals and monthly trends. There's no separate card reader to buy, pair, or charge — you take a payment by holding a contactless card or another phone to the back of yours.

<Note>
  The iOS app is a companion to Studio, not a replacement. You still add films, upload posters and stream files, and manage payouts on the web at [/account](https://usescreening.com/account). The app focuses on the things you actually do on the night, plus the shortcuts you want from your phone.
</Note>

## What the app does

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Door check-in" icon="qrcode">
    Search by name, email, or ticket code, or scan the QR code from a buyer's confirmation email. Admit tickets in one tap. The same one-admission rule as the web check-in tool applies — a re-scanned ticket is flagged as already checked in.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tap to Pay door sales" icon="credit-card">
    Sell a ticket at the door using the buyer's contactless card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay held to the back of your iPhone. The buyer's ticket lands in their email in seconds and is immediately admissible at the door.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Create a screening in the app" icon="calendar-plus">
    Tap the floating **+** button on any tab, pick a film from your library, set title, showtime, price, venue, and description, and post. Uses the same publish flow as Studio, so it shows up on your booth page as soon as you save.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Insights" icon="chart-line">
    Lifetime revenue, tickets sold, and screening count at a glance, with a six-month revenue trend and a per-screening breakdown. Backed by ticket-level data, so it matches Studio's Money view.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Requirements

Tap to Pay on iPhone is a Stripe and Apple feature with hard requirements. Check all of these before your first door shift.

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Device** | iPhone XS or later, running iOS 16.7 or newer |
| **Region** | Tap to Pay is available in the countries [Stripe supports](https://stripe.com/terminal/tap-to-pay) — currently including the UK, US, and most of Europe |
| **Payouts** | Your organisation must have finished Stripe Connect onboarding and be able to sell online. See [Stripe Connect setup](/payouts/stripe-connect) |
| **Team role** | The person taking payments needs a Studio role with door access — `owner`, `admin`, or `door` |
| **App install** | Download **Screening for Hosts** from the App Store |

<Warning>
  If your organisation isn't approved to sell, or Stripe Connect isn't finished, the Sell tab will show a **Payouts not ready** message and no charge can be taken. Finish onboarding on the web before the night.
</Warning>

## Signing in

Sign-in happens entirely inside the app — no browser handoff. Two paths are supported:

- **Sign in with Apple** — tap **Continue with Apple** and confirm with Face ID or Touch ID. Uses the email on your Apple ID to match your existing Screening host account. Your account must already have that email attached; the app does not create new host accounts.
- **Sign in with passkey** — if you've added a passkey to your account, tap **Sign in with passkey** and use Face ID or Touch ID. No password required.

<Tip>
  Add a passkey from your account settings on the web before your first door shift. It's the fastest way to sign staff in on venue Wi-Fi, and it removes shared passwords from your workflow.
</Tip>

The app keeps you signed in on that device. Sign out from the **Account** tab (a confirmation prompt appears before the session is revoked) — signing out invalidates that device's tokens on the server, so a lost or stolen phone stops working as soon as you tap it.

## Running door check-in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick tonight's screening">
    Open the app. The Screenings tab lists your upcoming nights. Tap the one you're running the door for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search or scan">
    Use the search field to type a name, email, or ticket code, or tap the QR icon to open the camera scanner. Point the camera at the buyer's QR code from their confirmation email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Admit">
    Tap **Admit**. The ticket is marked checked-in immediately. Rescanning the same ticket shows an **Already admitted** warning with the original check-in time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

All check-ins from the app appear alongside web check-ins in Studio — a mixed door team can safely use both. Door stats (sold, checked-in, remaining) refresh every time you return to the screening's Door screen, and you can pull down to refresh at any time.

## Live sales and check-in stats

Open a screening in the app to see the numbers you care about at a glance: **tickets sold**, **revenue**, **checked-in**, and **capacity**. Everything updates as sales come in from the web, from the box office, and from the app itself.

## Selling a ticket at the door

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Sell tab">
    On the screening detail screen, tap **Sell ticket**. The app confirms your Stripe Terminal reader is connected — this happens automatically the first time you open Sell for a given screening.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a tier and enter the buyer's email">
    Choose a ticket tier (Standard, Concession, or any custom tier you've added on the screening), set the quantity, and enter the buyer's email address. The email is required so their ticket and receipt can be sent. The tier picker charges the tier's real price — not a rounded or default rate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take the payment">
    Tap **Charge**. Hold the buyer's contactless card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay to the back of your iPhone until the confirmation chime plays.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirmation">
    On success, the buyer receives their ticket and receipt by email within a few seconds. The ticket is immediately admissible — you can hand the phone straight back and check them in from the same app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Door sales use the same fees, payouts, and Stripe Connect settings as your online sales. See [Fees explained](/payouts/fees-explained) for the fee schedule.

## Creating a screening from the app

Every tab in the app has a floating **+** button in the bottom-right corner. Tap it from anywhere to schedule a new night:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a film">
    The app lists the films already in your library. Choose the one you're screening — you can't upload a new film or stream file from the app, so add those on the web first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    Set the screening's public title, showtime, ticket price, venue, and description. These are the same fields as Studio's create-a-screening form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post">
    Tap **Save**. The screening is created via the same endpoint the web uses, so it appears in your Screenings list, in Insights, and on your booth page immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The app creates and edits the on-the-night details. Anything that isn't shown in the app — tiers beyond the default price, cover art, on-demand rent/buy pricing, share kit assets — is still edited from Studio on the web.
</Note>

## Insights

Open the **Insights** tab to see how you're doing across every screening you've ever run.

- **Lifetime totals** — total revenue, total tickets sold, and number of screenings.
- **6-month revenue trend** — a chart of monthly revenue for the last six months.
- **Per-screening breakdown** — one row per screening (newest first) with tickets sold, revenue, capacity, and status. Tap a row to open its screening detail screen.

Insights is calculated from ticket-level data (paid, non-revoked tickets in the `valid` or `used` states), so its numbers match Studio's Money view. Refunded or cancelled tickets are excluded.

## When sales are blocked

The Sell tab will refuse to take a payment in these cases:

| Reason | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Doors closed** — the screening has started and late sales are disabled | Enable late sales for the screening in Studio, or check the buyer in as a comp |
| **Sold out** — no tickets remaining for the tier | Nothing you can do at the door; the cap is set on the screening |
| **Payouts not ready** — Stripe Connect isn't complete or the org isn't approved | Finish Stripe Connect onboarding on the web |
| **Price too low** — the ticket price is lower than the platform fee | Raise the tier price in Studio |

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="QR scanning doesn't open the camera">
    Check that Camera permission is granted for **Screening for Hosts** in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Tap to Pay says the device isn't supported">
    Tap to Pay requires an iPhone XS or later on iOS 16.7 or newer, in a supported region. iPad is not supported.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The buyer says they didn't receive the ticket email">
    Ask them to check spam and promotional folders. The confirmation is sent from the same address as your online ticket confirmations. If it still hasn't arrived after a few minutes, resend it from the buyer's order in Studio.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Payment failed with a card decline">
    Ask the buyer to try a different card or payment method. Screening never sees the card details — the decline comes from the buyer's bank, not from Screening or Stripe.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
