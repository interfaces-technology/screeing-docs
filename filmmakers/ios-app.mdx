**Screening for Hosts** is the native iOS app for filmmakers and door staff. It signs in with your usual Studio account, pulls your upcoming screenings, and puts two on-the-night tools at your fingertips: door check-in (search or QR scan) and in-person ticket sales via **Tap to Pay on iPhone**. There's no separate reader to buy, pair, or charge — you take a payment by holding a contactless card or another phone to the back of yours.

<Note>
  The iOS app is a companion to Studio, not a replacement. You still create films, schedule screenings, and manage payouts on the web at [/account](https://usescreening.com/account). The app focuses on the two things you actually do at a venue.
</Note>

## What the app does

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Door check-in" icon="qrcode">
    Search by name, email, or ticket code, or scan the QR code from a buyer's confirmation email. Admit tickets in one tap. The same one-admission rule as the web check-in tool applies — a re-scanned ticket is flagged as already checked in.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tap to Pay door sales" icon="credit-card">
    Sell a ticket at the door using the buyer's contactless card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay held to the back of your iPhone. Their ticket lands in their email in seconds and is immediately admissible at the door.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Requirements

Tap to Pay on iPhone is a Stripe and Apple feature with hard requirements. Check all of these before your first door shift.

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Device** | iPhone XS or later, running iOS 16.7 or newer |
| **Region** | Tap to Pay is available in the countries [Stripe supports](https://stripe.com/terminal/tap-to-pay) — currently including the UK, US, and most of Europe |
| **Payouts** | Your organisation must have finished Stripe Connect onboarding and be able to sell online. See [Stripe Connect setup](/payouts/stripe-connect) |
| **Team role** | The person taking payments needs a Studio role with door access — `owner`, `admin`, or `door` |
| **App install** | Download **Screening for Hosts** from the App Store |

<Warning>
  If your organisation isn't approved to sell, or Stripe Connect isn't finished, the Sell tab will show a **Payouts not ready** message and no charge can be taken. Finish onboarding on the web before the night.
</Warning>

## Signing in

The app uses the same sign-in as Studio. Two paths are supported:

- **Passkey sign-in** — if you've added a passkey to your account, tap **Sign in with passkey** and use Face ID or Touch ID. No password required and no browser opens.
- **Web sign-in fallback** — tap **Sign in** to open a secure Apple system browser to `usescreening.com/login`. Sign in as normal (password or passkey), and the app is returned to and signed in automatically.

<Tip>
  Add a passkey from your account settings on the web before your first door shift. It's the fastest way to sign staff in on venue Wi-Fi, and it removes shared passwords from your workflow.
</Tip>

The app keeps you signed in on that device. Sign out from the **Account** tab to revoke the device.

## Running door check-in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick tonight's screening">
    Open the app. The Screenings tab lists your upcoming nights. Tap the one you're running the door for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search or scan">
    Use the search field to type a name, email, or ticket code, or tap the QR icon to open the camera scanner. Point the camera at the buyer's QR code from their confirmation email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Admit">
    Tap **Admit**. The ticket is marked checked-in immediately. Rescanning the same ticket shows an **Already admitted** warning with the original check-in time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

All check-ins from the app appear alongside web check-ins in Studio — a mixed door team can safely use both.

## Selling a ticket at the door

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Sell tab">
    On the screening detail screen, tap **Sell ticket**. The app confirms your Stripe Terminal reader is connected — this happens automatically the first time you open Sell for a given screening.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a tier and enter the buyer's email">
    Choose a ticket tier if you have more than one, set the quantity, and enter the buyer's email address. The email is required so their ticket and receipt can be sent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take the payment">
    Tap **Charge**. Hold the buyer's contactless card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay to the back of your iPhone until the confirmation chime plays.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirmation">
    On success, the buyer receives their ticket and receipt by email within a few seconds. The ticket is immediately admissible — you can hand the phone straight back and check them in from the same app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Door sales use the same fees, payouts, and Stripe Connect settings as your online sales. See [Fees explained](/payouts/fees-explained) for the fee schedule.

## When sales are blocked

The Sell tab will refuse to take a payment in these cases:

| Reason | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Doors closed** — the screening has started and late sales are disabled | Enable late sales for the screening in Studio, or check the buyer in as a comp |
| **Sold out** — no tickets remaining for the tier | Nothing you can do at the door; the cap is set on the screening |
| **Payouts not ready** — Stripe Connect isn't complete or the org isn't approved | Finish Stripe Connect onboarding on the web |
| **Price too low** — the ticket price is lower than the platform fee | Raise the tier price in Studio |

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="QR scanning doesn't open the camera">
    Check that Camera permission is granted for **Screening for Hosts** in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Tap to Pay says the device isn't supported">
    Tap to Pay requires an iPhone XS or later on iOS 16.7 or newer, in a supported region. iPad is not supported.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The buyer says they didn't receive the ticket email">
    Ask them to check spam and promotional folders. The confirmation is sent from the same address as your online ticket confirmations. If it still hasn't arrived after a few minutes, resend it from the buyer's order in Studio.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Payment failed with a card decline">
    Ask the buyer to try a different card or payment method. Screening never sees the card details — the decline comes from the buyer's bank, not from Screening or Stripe.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
