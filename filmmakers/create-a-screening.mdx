A screening is a timed event attached to a film. You create the film once, then schedule one or more nights from it — an opening premiere, a second run, an online encore. Each night has its own showtime, format, ticket pricing, and capacity. New screenings always start as a **Draft**, which shows a public Coming Soon page but does not open ticket sales until you publish.

## Create a screening night

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Films in Studio">
    Go to [/account](https://usescreening.com/account) and select **Films** in the left navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the film">
    Click the film you want to schedule a night for. If you haven't added the film yet, [add it first](/filmmakers/add-a-film).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new night">
    On the film detail page, click **New night**. The screening form opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the form">
    Set the format, showtime, ticket tiers, capacity, and any additional options described on this page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save as draft">
    Click **Save as draft**. Your screening is created with Coming Soon status. Buyers can see it on Discover if you've listed it, but cannot buy tickets yet. [Publish separately](/filmmakers/publish-and-share) when you're ready to open sales.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Screening formats

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Online">
    An online screening is stream-only. Buyers receive a link to watch at `usescreening.com/{org}/{slug}/live` during the live window. There is no door entry — no QR code is issued and no venue details are shown.

    **Requirements before publish:**
    - Stream file uploaded and processed (Mux)
    - Rights attested on the film record
    - Stripe connected

    Buyers need only their purchase email to access the stream.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="In Person">
    An in-person screening is door-entry only. Buyers receive a confirmation email with a QR code and ticket code. Door staff scan or search the code on the night using the Door view in Studio.

    **Requirements before publish:**
    - Venue name and address filled in
    - Stripe connected

    No stream is served for in-person tickets. The `/live` route is not active.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Hybrid">
    A hybrid screening gives buyers the choice at checkout: attend in the room or watch the stream online. Both audiences see the same showtime. In-person buyers get a QR code; online buyers get a stream link. Revenue from both ticket types flows into the same screening.

    **Requirements before publish:**
    - Stream file uploaded and processed (Mux)
    - Venue name and address filled in
    - Rights attested on the film record
    - Stripe connected

    <Note>
      The QR code does not unlock the stream, and the stream link does not work at the door. The two ticket types are separate.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Showtime and runtime

Set the **start time** for your screening. Screening uses the film's runtime (set on the film record) to calculate the end of the live window automatically. The close window is: start time + film runtime + a configurable **close-late buffer** (a grace period after the credits). You can adjust the buffer on the screening form.

## Premieres and encores

Each film can have one premiere night. Tick **This is a premiere** on the form to mark the opening night. Premiere is a label. It appears as a badge on the booth, on Discover, and in emails. It is not a separate product or a different ticket type.

Once a film has a premiere night, **New night** opens the form as an **Encore**. An encore is another showing of the film, and buyers see it as an encore rather than a second premiere. If you try to mark a second night as a premiere, Studio rejects it with the message "This film already has a premiere night. Schedule an encore instead."

### Premiere shape

When you schedule a premiere, you can optionally pick a **Premiere shape**. Each shape pre-fills the form with a suggested format, capacity, and venue name. You can still change every field afterwards.

| Shape | Best for | Format | Suggested capacity | Venue name placeholder |
|-------|----------|--------|--------------------|------------------------|
| **Living room premiere** | An intimate room plus friends online. A low-stakes first audience. | Hybrid | 30 | Your living room or studio |
| **Community hall** | A local venue with a stream for diaspora and press. | Hybrid | 120 | Community hall, arts centre, or library |
| **Cinema + world** | A pop-up or indie cinema with a global online night. | Hybrid | 200 | Cinema or screening room |

Studio only fills the venue name if the field is empty. Replace the placeholder with your real venue name and add the address before you publish. If you change the format by hand after picking a shape, Studio clears the shape selection.

## Ticket tiers

Each screening supports two ticket tiers out of the box:

| Tier | Typical use |
|------|------------|
| **Standard** | Full-price admission |
| **Concession** | Discounted rate — students, unwaged, OAPs |

Set a price and an optional capacity for each tier independently. Leave a tier's capacity blank to make it unlimited within the overall screening cap.

## Capacity

Set an overall capacity for the screening, a per-tier cap, or both. When a tier sells out, its buy button disappears from the booth. When the overall cap is reached, the booth shows the screening as sold out.

<Note>
  Capacity is optional. If you leave it blank, Screening will not cap ticket sales automatically.
</Note>

## Late sales

By default, ticket sales close at the screening's start time. Toggle **Late sales** on if you want to keep the checkout open after the show has started — useful for online screenings where latecomers can still join mid-stream.

## Venue details (in-person and hybrid)

For in-person and hybrid screenings, add:

- **Venue name** — shown on the booth and in buyer confirmation emails
- **Venue address** — shown on the booth and included in the calendar `.ics` download

These fields are required before you can publish an in-person or hybrid screening.

## Interest and waitlist

Before your tickets are on sale, you can collect demand by enabling the **Interest** toggle on the screening form. This creates a public page at `usescreening.com/interest/{slug}` where visitors can register their interest without paying.

Switch off Interest and publish the screening when you're ready to open sales — registered interest is not automatically converted to tickets.

## Recurring series

To run the same screening on a repeating schedule, enable **Recurring** on the form and set an interval. Screening creates individual night records for each occurrence, each with its own ticket sales and check-in.

## CMS status reference

| Status | Buyer sees | Sales open |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| **Draft** | Coming Soon page | No |
| **Published** | Full booth with buy button | Yes |
| **Cancelled** | Closed booth | No |

New screenings always start as Draft. [Publish from Studio → Nights](/filmmakers/publish-and-share) when you're ready to go live.
