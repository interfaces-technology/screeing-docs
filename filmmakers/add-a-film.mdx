Films are the canonical entity in Screening. You create a film record once, then schedule as many screening nights as you like from it — an opening night, a second run, an online encore. Everything about the film — its poster, stream file, and on-demand pricing — lives in one place and applies to every night you create from it. Start by adding the film to your Studio catalogue.

## Add a film

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Films section">
    In Studio at [/account](https://usescreening.com/account), select **Films** in the left navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new film">
    Click **New film**. A form opens for the film's details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the film details">
    Enter the required and optional fields described below. At minimum you need a title and a runtime.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the stream file">
    If you're planning an online or hybrid screening, upload the film file now. Scroll to the **Stream** section and follow the upload prompt. Select your video file and Screening handles transcoding automatically — no separate upload service required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the film">
    Click **Save**. Your film appears in the Films catalogue. You can edit it at any time before or after scheduling nights.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Film fields

### Required

| Field | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| **Title** | The public name of the film, shown on the booth, confirmation emails, and the film page. |
| **Runtime (minutes)** | How long the film runs. Screening uses this to calculate the end of the live stream window — for example, a 90-minute film that starts at 19:30 closes the stream at approximately 21:00, plus any close-late buffer you configure. |

### Optional

| Field | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| **Director** | Shown on the booth and film page. |
| **Year** | The production or release year. |
| **Poster image** | Recommended aspect ratio is 2:3 (standard film poster). Appears on the booth, Discover, and the film page. |
| **Film still** | A landscape image used as a banner or background. |

### Rights attestation

Before you can publish any screening using this film, you must tick the **rights attestation** checkbox on the film record — a confirmation that you hold the distribution rights. You can tick it when you first create the film or any time before you publish. Studio will prompt you again at publish time if it hasn't been completed.

<Note>
  The rights attestation is required before publishing. You'll see a prompt on the film record and a blocking check at publish time if it hasn't been ticked.
</Note>

## Uploading the stream file

Screening delivers your film through [Mux](https://mux.com) using signed playback tokens — the raw video file is never exposed directly to the browser. Upload the file once and Screening handles transcoding and delivery for every screening and on-demand order you create from this film.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the film record">
    In **Studio → Films**, click the film you want to add a stream to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Stream section">
    Scroll down to the **Stream** section on the film detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the upload">
    Click **Upload film file** and select your video file. The upload goes directly from your browser — you don't need to upload to a separate service first. Files of any size are supported.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for processing">
    Mux transcodes your file after upload. This usually takes a few minutes for a feature-length film. The film record will show a ready status when transcoding is complete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Online and hybrid screenings require a ready stream file before you can publish. If the file is still processing when you try to publish, Studio will block the action and show a status message.
</Warning>

## On-demand options

After a screening night ends, you can keep the film available for viewers to rent or buy. Set these options on the film record — they apply to the film's catalog page at `usescreening.com/{org}/film/{film-slug}`.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Rent" icon="clock">
    Set a rental price and a rental duration in hours. When a buyer pays, the access window opens immediately and counts down from the moment of purchase. After the window closes, they need to rent again to watch.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Buy" icon="lock-open">
    Set a buy price for permanent access. Buyers who purchase the film keep access to it indefinitely under the same email address used at checkout.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  You don't need to set on-demand options right away. Configure them whenever you're ready for the film to be available between or after your screening nights.
</Tip>

## Film slug and catalog URL

When you create a film, Screening generates a URL slug from the title. The film's public catalog page lives at:

```
usescreening.com/{your-org}/film/{film-slug}
```

You can customise the slug on the film record before publishing. Once buyers have purchased or rented via a URL, avoid changing the slug — it will break any existing links.
