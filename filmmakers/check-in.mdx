Screening's door check-in tool is web-based — there's no app to install. Any member of your team with the `door` role can check in buyers from any device with a browser, whether that's a phone at the door, a tablet at the desk, or a laptop backstage. Buyers show their QR code or ticket code from their confirmation email, and you mark them admitted in seconds.

<Note>
  Door staff can use any device with a modern browser — no app installation needed. Share the Studio link ([/account](https://usescreening.com/account)) with your door team before the night.
</Note>

## Before the night: invite door staff

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Team tab">
    In Studio at [/account](https://usescreening.com/account), select **Team** in the left navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your door staff">
    Click **Invite**, enter the team member's email address, and set their role to **Door**. Send the invite.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Door staff accept the invite">
    Your team member receives an invite email. They click the link, create a password (or use a passkey), and land on the Door view in Studio. They see only the Door section — no films, no sales data, no audience list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Invite door staff a day or two before the screening so they can confirm access and log in before the night begins.
</Tip>

## On the night: checking in buyers

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Door in Studio">
    Sign in to Studio at [/account](https://usescreening.com/account) on your check-in device. Select **Door** in the navigation. You'll see the check-in interface for your active screening.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the screening">
    If you have multiple nights, select the correct screening from the list. Only screenings scheduled for today are shown by default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check in a buyer">
    Use one of two methods:

    - **Search by ticket code** — type or paste the buyer's ticket code into the search field. The buyer's name and ticket details appear. Click **Admit** to check them in.
    - **Scan QR code** — click **Scan QR** to activate your device's camera. Hold the buyer's QR code in front of the camera. Screening reads the code and admits the ticket automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm admission">
    Once admitted, the ticket is marked as checked in. If a buyer tries to use the same ticket code or QR again, the system flags it as already admitted.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## QR codes and ticket types

Buyers receive their QR code in their confirmation email when they purchase an **in-person** or **hybrid (in-person)** ticket. Not all ticket types include a QR code:

| Ticket type | QR code issued | Check-in method |
|-------------|---------------|----------------|
| **In-person** | ✅ Yes | Scan QR or search ticket code |
| **Hybrid — in-person** | ✅ Yes | Scan QR or search ticket code |
| **Hybrid — online** | ❌ No | Buyers use their stream link at `/live` |
| **Online only** | ❌ No | Buyers use their stream link — no door entry |

<Note>
  The QR code at the door and the stream link are separate access methods. Scanning a QR code at the door does not grant access to the online stream, and having a stream link does not allow entry to the physical venue.
</Note>

## Hybrid screenings

For hybrid screenings, check in only the buyers who chose the **in-person** ticket type at checkout. Online ticket holders access the stream directly at `usescreening.com/{org}/{slug}/live` using the link in their confirmation email — they do not come through the door.

## One check-in per ticket

Each ticket can only be admitted once. If the same QR code or ticket code is scanned a second time, Screening shows an **Already admitted** message with the original check-in time. This prevents ticket sharing on the door.

## Roles and access

The `door` role is intentionally limited. A team member with the door role can:

- View the Door check-in interface
- Search and admit tickets for any active screening in the organisation

They cannot:

- View the Films, Nights, People, or Money sections
- Edit or publish screenings
- See sales data or audience information

This means you can safely hand a check-in device to a volunteer without giving them access to your business data.
