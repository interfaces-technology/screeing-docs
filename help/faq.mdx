Find answers to the most common questions about buying tickets, watching screenings, and running your own premiere on Screening. Questions are split into two sections — one for buyers and one for filmmakers.

## For buyers

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I haven't received my confirmation email">
    First, check your spam or junk folder — confirmation emails occasionally land there depending on your email provider. If you don't find it there, visit [usescreening.com/me](https://usescreening.com/me) and enter the email address you used at checkout to look up your tickets directly. Your ticket and QR code are accessible there even without a Screening account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I get a refund?">
    All Screening ticket sales are final. Screenings are events with a fixed showtime, and the platform does not support buyer-initiated refunds. If you believe there is a genuine exceptional issue — for example, a technical problem that prevented you from watching — contact the filmmaker directly or reach out to Screening support at [usescreening.com](https://usescreening.com). Refunds are not guaranteed and are handled case by case.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The live stream won't load">
    Try the following steps in order:
    1. Make sure you're accessing the stream using the **signed ticket link** from your confirmation email — this is the most reliable way to verify your ticket.
    2. Check that the **showtime window is currently open**. Streams go live at the scheduled start time; visiting the page early shows a "Starting soon" screen.
    3. Try a **different browser** — Chrome and Firefox are most reliable for Mux-powered streams.
    4. Disable any VPN or browser extensions that might interfere with video playback.
    5. If none of the above works, visit [usescreening.com/me](https://usescreening.com/me) with your purchase email to confirm your ticket is valid, then contact Screening support.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I bought an in-person ticket but now I can't attend">
    All Screening ticket sales are final. There is no transfer, exchange, or refund feature for in-person tickets. You may be able to pass your ticket code directly to someone else to attend in your place — door staff check in by code or QR, not by name. Contact Screening support if you need assistance with an exceptional situation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need an account to buy a ticket?">
    No. Tickets are tied to the email address you provide at checkout — you don't need a Screening account to purchase or attend. If you'd like to keep all your tickets and watch history in one place, you can create a free account at [usescreening.com/me](https://usescreening.com/me) using the same email address.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I watch on multiple devices at the same time?">
    No. Only one device can stream at a time per ticket email. If you open the stream on a second device, that device becomes the active session and your first device is disconnected. To resume on your original device, close the stream on the second device and reload the first.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## For filmmakers

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I get host access?">
    Screening is **invite-only** for hosts. Submit a hosting request at [usescreening.com/host](https://usescreening.com/host). The Screening team reviews requests and sends an invite when your application is approved. There is no automated approval — the team manually vets each new filmmaker to maintain platform quality.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My screening won't publish — what am I missing?">
    Before a screening can be published, you need to complete the publish checklist:
    1. **Film file uploaded** — for online and hybrid screenings, the film must be uploaded and processed by Mux before you can go live.
    2. **Rights attested** — you must confirm you hold the rights to screen the film at publish time.
    3. **Stripe connected and verified** — for paid screenings, your Stripe account must have `card_payments` enabled. See [Connect Stripe](/payouts/stripe-connect) for setup steps.

    Studio will show which checklist items are outstanding. Free (£0) screenings don't require Stripe to be connected.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When do I receive my payout?">
    Stripe Express pays out on a **rolling 2–7 day schedule** for most countries. Funds collected from ticket sales are typically in your bank account within that window after the sale. New accounts may have a longer initial holding period while Stripe establishes account history. Check your exact payout schedule by clicking **Open Stripe Dashboard** in Studio → Money.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I offer refunds to buyers?">
    There is no host refund interface in Studio — all Screening ticket sales are final. If a buyer contacts you about an exceptional issue, ask them to reach out to Screening support directly. Any refund actions are handled through the Stripe Dashboard on a case-by-case basis and are not guaranteed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I add a team member?">
    Go to **Studio → Team** and click **Invite**. Enter the team member's email address and assign them a role:
    - **Owner** — full access including billing and account deletion
    - **Admin** — full Studio access including Money; can invite other members
    - **Editor** — can manage films and screenings, cannot view Money or Team
    - **Door** — can only access the door check-in view on the night

    Invitees receive an email with a link to accept and set up their access.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I run a free screening?">
    Yes. Set the ticket price to **£0** when creating your screening. Free screenings collect no payment and incur no Screening fee. Buyers still go through the normal checkout flow and receive a confirmation email with a ticket code — they just don't enter any card details. Free screenings also do not require a connected Stripe account to publish.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
